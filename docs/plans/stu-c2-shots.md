# STU-C2: headshot and cowboy shot of an OC sheet's character (2026-10-09)

Goal: from a finished OC sheet, one click makes a headshot (neck up) or a cowboy shot (thighs up) of the
same character.

## What it does

- Workflow `studio/workflows/oc-shots` (generated by `tools/refine/gen.py`, section 6), a refine step
  (`[studio] refine = true`, `[inputs] source = "10"`): the source is the sheet.
- The sheet is scaled to 3024x1512 and cropped twice: the front cell (0,0 756x1512: outfit, colours,
  accessories) and the front head (208,80 320x320: face, hair, moustache, headphones). Both go as one batch into
  IP-Adapter plus (`likeness`, default 0.7, end 0.8). Marvel Rivals style LoRA 0.8 like the sheet; the
  character-sheet LoRA is left out (it draws turnarounds).
- Same prompt, seed and face prompt as the sheet; only the shot tags change (`shot` = headshot / cowboy, with
  `{shot_tags}` / `{shot_neg}`). The face prompt is concatenated to the prompt and drives the face fix, so a
  moustache named there stays. Sizes: headshot `tall` 1024x1280, cowboy `square` 1216x1216; 4x-AnimeSharp and a
  0.5 scale give 2048x2560 / 2432x2432.
- Kreative Studio: OC sheet cards have Headshot and Cowboy shot buttons (instead of Animate); one click posts
  `/api/refine` with the sheet as the source and the server fills prompt, seed, rating, face prompt and
  background from the sheet's job (new sheets now keep rating and params on their job; older ones fall back to
  general / empty face prompt / white). Finished shots show as small thumbnails under their sheet
  (`refineOf`). The Refine tab has Headshot and Cowboy steps too (uploads need a description).

## Findings (soucouyant RX 9070 XT)

| Run | Result | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Two IP-Adapters (plus on the cell 0.6, plus-face on the head 0.7), 1024x1280 | out of VRAM in the KSampler (13.5 GB torch) | - |
| One IP-Adapter plus on both crops (ImageBatch), 0.7 | fits | 79-92 s |
| Loquendo headshot / cowboy, 0.7 | monocle, headphones, purple eyes, tie, white suit with purple; moustache thinner than the sheet's, hair longer | 92 / 84 s |
| Teal knight headshot / cowboy, 0.7 | helmet and visor, teal sword; armour drifts to white and teal (the prompt says white and gold; the sheet drew black and gold) | 87 / 79 s |
| Teal knight cowboy, 0.85 | black and gold like the sheet, sword kept: best | 91 s |
| Teal knight cowboy, 1.0 | the look of the sheet but the sword and pose are lost | 86 s |
| Loquendo headshot, 0.85 | monocle lost, face more generic: 0.7 is better for faces | 92 s |

Default stays 0.7; "Outfit likeness" in the Refine tab raises it for armour.
Direct ComfyUI tests: POST /free acts only when ComfyUI's worker loop wakes, so wait until `system_stats`
shows `torch_vram_total` near 0 before queueing; otherwise the previous graph's models stay loaded.

## Not done here

- Kompanion's own Studio page has no refine steps (STU-R2 put them in Kreative Studio), so shots are made from
  Kreative Studio; a "Headshot / Cowboy shot" on Kompanion's run cards needs a server-side "source = this run"
  and is left for STU-UX1.
- The face crop assumes the oc-sheet v2 template (front figure in the left quarter, head at the top); a classic
  sheet or an uploaded picture gets a poorer face reference.
