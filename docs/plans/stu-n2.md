# STU-N2: multi-person VN scenes (work in progress, 2026-10-12)

Workflows `vn-cast-2/3/4` (generated by `tools/studio/gen_cast.py`): a scene prompt over the whole picture plus one text encode per person, each limited to a vertical strip
(ConditioningSetAreaPercentage, strips overlap 7 %) and joined with ConditioningCombine; every person's text starts with `adult, mature, solo`, the child guard stays in the negative.
Studio "VN scene, cast" card with a Cast panel (2 to 4 people, left to right, move/add/remove) picks the workflow by the number of people.
